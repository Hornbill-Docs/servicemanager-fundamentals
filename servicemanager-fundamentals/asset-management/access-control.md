# Access Control

## Controlling who can see and do what

Access to asset information is controlled through Hornbill's role-based security model, with a dedicated Asset Management role governing who can view, create, update, or delete asset records, and asset partitions providing a further, more granular layer of access control at the level of individual groups of assets.

**Learn more:** [Service Manager User Roles](https://docs.hornbill.com/servicemanager-config/setup/service-manager-roles) | [Assets Overview](https://docs.hornbill.com/servicemanager-user-guide/asset-management/overview)
