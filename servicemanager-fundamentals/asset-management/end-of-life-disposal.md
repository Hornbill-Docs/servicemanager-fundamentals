# End of Life and Disposal

## Preparing assets for disposal

Predisposal activities - such as software license harvesting, data wiping, or reconditioning - can be tracked using workflow tasks and updates captured against the asset record, with the flexibility to define your own set of predisposal actions to match your organization's process.

**Learn more:** [Requests Automation](https://docs.hornbill.com/servicemanager-config/customize/workflows/requests-automation)

## Recording how assets were disposed of

Asset records include dedicated disposal fields - including disposal reason and disposal price - which can be configured with your own client-specific disposal methods (self-disposed, disposal vendor, sold, donated, returned to lessor, lost, or stolen), and supporting certificates of destruction or disposal can be linked to the asset record through Hornbill Document Management.

**Learn more:** [Asset Structure](https://docs.hornbill.com/servicemanager-user-guide/asset-management/asset-structure) | [Hornbill Document Management](https://www.hornbill.com/products/document-management)

## Linking critical documents to assets

Documents can be linked directly to an asset record and are visible as part of the asset's relationships in the Configuration Item Explorer, giving you a way to validate that required documents - contracts, compliance certificates, retention records - exist for a given asset type or individual asset, and to reach them directly from the asset itself.

**Learn more:** [Configuration Item Explorer](https://docs.hornbill.com/servicemanager-user-guide/configuration-management/configuration-item-explorer) | [Hornbill Document Management](https://www.hornbill.com/products/document-management)
