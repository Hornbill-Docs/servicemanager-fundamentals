# Software and Mobile Asset Management

## Software asset management and license compliance

Hornbill's Software Asset Management view gives you a breakdown of installed software by product, vendor, and version across your devices, cross-referenced against the license records held in your asset repository, to help you understand license compliance and identify unused or underutilized software.

**Learn more:** [Software Asset Management](https://docs.hornbill.com/servicemanager-user-guide/asset-management/software-asset-management)

## Mobile asset and device management

Through iBridge, Hornbill integrates directly with Microsoft Intune, enabling mobile and managed devices to be wiped or remotely locked from a Hornbill workflow - useful for lost or stolen devices, or as part of a structured leaver or decommissioning process. Broader mobile device inventory, compliance, and software push are handled by your MDM/MAM platform itself, with Hornbill providing the asset record, workflow, and request context around it.

**Learn more:** [Microsoft Intune Integration](https://docs.hornbill.com/ibridge-packages/microsoft/intune) | [Intune: Wipe Device](https://docs.hornbill.com/ibridge-packages/microsoft/intune/wipedevice) | [Intune: Remote Lock Device](https://docs.hornbill.com/ibridge-packages/microsoft/intune/remotelockdevice)
