# Subscription Model

Hornbill offers a subscription-based pricing model for its Service Manager application, which is designed to provide flexibility and scalability for organizations of all sizes. The subscription model allows customers to choose the level of access and features they need, while also providing the option to scale up or down as their requirements change.

![Subscription Model](/_books/servicemanager-fundamentals/images/subscription-model.png)

## Service Management User Types

|User Type|Subscription|What they can do|
|---|---|---|
|Platform Users|Paid|Authentication, authorizations, Hornbill app enablement and workflow|
|Self-Service Users|Free|End-user access to raise requests, track progress and view knowledge articles|
|Named Service Manager Agents|Paid|Guaranteed, dedicated access to the Service Manager application|
|Concurrent Service Manager Agents|Paid|Shared access to the Service Manager application|
