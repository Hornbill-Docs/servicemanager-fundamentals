# Welcome to Service Manager Fundamentals
Hornbill Service Manager is a cloud-based solution that can help your organization with workflow and automation capabilities to support your Enterprise Service Management (ESM) and Digital Transformation initiatives. The platform has a very wide range of functional capabilities, and this guide is designed to help you understand the fundamental high-level concepts, capabilities of the Hornbill platform, without going into any specific technical details.  This guide focuses on the "why" and "what", and not on the "how."

Thank you for your interest and your time in discovering more about what the Hornbill Platform has to offer. 

## Intended Audience 
This document should be used by anyone who will be involved in the selection of Hornbill Service Manager as their service management solution, or anyone working with Hornbill Service Manager on a day-to-day basis, especially those users who are involved in deploying, administering and getting business value from the use of the Hornbill Service Manager within their organization.

You should consider understanding the fundamentals of Service Manager if you are: -

- Evaluating Hornbill Service Manager to use as your system of record/system of action that will underpin your IT Service Management, Enterprise Service Management (ESM) and Digital Transformation initiatives.
- Considering using Hornbill Service Manager within your company or organization.
- Implementing, Delivering or Advising on the use of Hornbill Service Manager internally to other business units, teams, departments or to other organizations.
- Day to Day administration and power-user use of Hornbill Service Manager.
- Customizing/Building Service Management related Intelligent Captures, Workflows and Automation's
- you are new to Hornbill Service Manager and you wish to know more about what Hornbill Service Manager is, and what its capabilities are. 

This guide will be valuable to individuals who work with (or are considering using) Hornbill Service Manager within their organization: -

- Service Delivery Managers
- Service Desk Managers
- IT Directors 
- CTO's and/or CIO's responsible for applications selection
- System Administrators and/or Power Users of Hornbill Service Manager
- Consultants engaging in guidance, training, implementation or support of solutions based on Hornbill Service Manager
