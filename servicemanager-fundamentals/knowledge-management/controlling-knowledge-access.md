# Controlling Who Sees and Does What

## The right knowledge for the right audience

A new knowledge base is open to everyone with the Knowledge User role by default, and you restrict it by adding subscriptions. A subscription can be to a service, so that everyone who has that service can see the knowledge base, or to a team, which is ideal for technical knowledge that's meant for support staff rather than end users. In practice this means audiences such as end users, service desk staff, IT teams, and power users each see only the knowledge meant for them. Hornbill describes these as separate knowledge bases, each acting as a dedicated library for a particular audience.

**Learn more:** [Subscriptions](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/knowledge-bases/subscriptions) | [Knowledge Roles](/servicemanager-config/setup/service-manager-roles#knowledge-roles)

## Roles for managing and contributing

Hornbill provides dedicated knowledge roles: Knowledge Administrator, Knowledge Manager (who owns and manages a knowledge base), Knowledge Contributor, and Knowledge User. Knowledge Managers can grant contributor access to named teams or users on a knowledge base, independently of who can read it, so you could let a whole department contribute content and edits. Roles are applied at the knowledge base level, and Hornbill also supports creating custom roles. There isn't a separate "subject matter expert" or "quality reviewer" role as such; review and approval is handled by the knowledge base owner.
