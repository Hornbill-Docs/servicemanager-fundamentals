# Lifecycle and Governance

## A simple, controlled publishing lifecycle

Each knowledge base has a setting to turn approvals on. When it's on, a contributor creates an article as a Draft, submits it for approval, and the knowledge base owner is notified. The owner can accept the article, which publishes it, or reject it, which returns it to draft and notifies the author, who can edit and resubmit. Articles created by the owner skip the approval step. Hornbill's lifecycle is deliberately straightforward: it does not offer client-defined statuses or multi-stage review chains, so organizations that need peer review, quality review, and scheduled review stages would handle those through process and workflow around the article.

**Learn more:** [Approvals](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/knowledge-bases/articles/approvals) | [Activity and Audit Log](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/knowledge-bases/activity-and-audit)

## Scheduled article reviews

Knowledge managers and contributors can add a review to any article, giving it a title, details, and a review date. The review is scheduled as an activity on that date, as a reminder to check whether the article is still valid, whether updates are needed, or how the article has been performing. Reviews appear in the article's review list and in the person's My Activities view. Reviews are set per article rather than calculated automatically from the publish date.

**Learn more:** [Article Reviews](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/knowledge-bases/articles/reviews)

## Change history and version control

Every change to a knowledge base and its articles is recorded in an audit trail that shows what was changed, including status changes from one value to another, and each knowledge base has an activity stream for discussion. This gives knowledge managers a clear record of who changed what and when. Hornbill does not maintain separate live, draft, and retired versions of the same article side by side, so organizations that need that would manage it through the approval process and the audit history.

**Learn more:** [Activity and Audit Log](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/knowledge-bases/activity-and-audit)

## Knowledge in multiple languages

Hornbill supports 173 languages across the platform, and administrators control which languages are available to users. The documentation reviewed doesn't describe a dedicated feature for publishing translated versions of a single article, so organizations that need this typically maintain language-specific articles or knowledge bases for their audiences. It's worth confirming the current options with your Hornbill contact if multilingual knowledge is a firm requirement.

**Learn more:** [Supported Languages](/esp-config/internationalization/supported-languages)
