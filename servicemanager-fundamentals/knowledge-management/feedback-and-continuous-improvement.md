# Feedback and Continuous Improvement

## Measuring how useful knowledge is

Hornbill captures metrics such as article views, likes, dislikes, and shares, and the Knowledge dashboard presents charts for articles per knowledge base, the most viewed articles, the most liked articles, and the most searched terms, which can be filtered by time and source. Together these show which content is working and which is not.

**Learn more:** [Knowledge Dashboard](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/dashboard) | [Search/Browse](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/search-and-browse)

## Spotting gaps and missing knowledge

Search logs show what people are looking for, including the most searched terms, which helps you spot topics where knowledge is missing or hard to find. Each knowledge base has an activity stream where managers and contributors can suggest improvements or point out mistakes.

**Learn more:** [Knowledge Dashboard](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/dashboard) | [Activity and Audit Log](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/knowledge-bases/activity-and-audit)

## Flagging articles that need attention

The dashboards and reports described above make it straightforward to see which articles are performing poorly, and knowledge managers can then schedule a review on the article itself.

**Learn more:** [Knowledge Dashboard](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/dashboard) | [Reports](/servicemanager-user-guide/reports/reports)

## Quick access to favorite articles

Full Hornbill users can bookmark favorite articles using the bookmark feature. Users reach the content they use most through global search, topic browsing, and the portal widgets that promote popular articles.

**Learn more:** [Search/Browse](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/search-and-browse) | [Welcome to Hornbill Knowledge](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/overview/welcome)
