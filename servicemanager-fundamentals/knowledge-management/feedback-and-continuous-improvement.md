# Feedback and Continuous Improvement

## Measuring how useful knowledge is

Hornbill captures metrics such as article views, likes, dislikes, and shares, and the Knowledge dashboard presents charts for articles per knowledge base, the most viewed articles, the most liked articles, and the most searched terms, which can be filtered by time and source. Together these show which content is working and which is not. Feedback is captured as likes and dislikes rather than rating scales or survey-style questions.

**Learn more:** [Knowledge Dashboard](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/dashboard) | [Search/Browse](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/search-and-browse)

## Spotting gaps and missing knowledge

Search data shows what people are looking for, including the most searched terms, which helps you spot topics where knowledge is missing or hard to find. Hornbill's Knowledge datasheet also describes knowledge nomination, where anyone can nominate an item for inclusion in the knowledge base, and each knowledge base has an activity stream where managers and contributors can suggest improvements or point out mistakes. A dedicated option for an end user to request an update or clarification on an article isn't described in the documentation reviewed.

**Learn more:** [Knowledge Dashboard](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/dashboard) | [Activity and Audit Log](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/knowledge-bases/activity-and-audit) | [Knowledge Datasheet](https://www.hornbill.com/hubfs/Knowledge_v1.2_2024.pdf)

## Flagging articles that need attention

Hornbill doesn't automatically flag articles for review when they cross a defined threshold. Instead, the dashboards and reports described above make it straightforward to see which articles are performing poorly, and knowledge managers can then schedule a review on the article itself. Where automatic flagging is a firm requirement, it would typically be built using reporting or workflow, so it's worth confirming with your Hornbill contact.

**Learn more:** [Knowledge Dashboard](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/dashboard) | [Reports](/servicemanager-user-guide/reports/reports)

## Quick access to favorite articles

The documentation reviewed doesn't describe a bookmarking, favorites, or personal reading-history feature for articles. Users reach the content they use most through global search, topic browsing, and the portal widgets that promote popular articles.
