# Reporting and Continuous Improvement

## Measuring first-call resolution

Hornbill's reporting and dashboards let you define, measure, and report on first-call resolution as its own metric, independent of how an incident is prioritized, so you can track how efficiently your service desk resolves issues at first contact.

**Learn more:** [Reports](/servicemanager-user-guide/reports/reports) | [My Dashboards](/servicemanager-user-guide/request-list/my-dashboard)

## Capturing and reporting support costs

Time and effort spent resolving incidents can be captured using Hornbill's Timesheet Manager, giving cost center managers visibility of support effort for individual incidents or in aggregate over time. This data can then feed into your existing finance or invoicing processes where required.

**Learn more:** Timesheet Manager Configuration Guide

## Post-incident reviews

Using views, dashboards, and reports built around your own criteria, you can identify incidents that should trigger a post-incident review — such as major incidents, incidents against particular services or users, or incidents where targets were missed — helping to drive continuous improvement.

**Learn more:** [Reports](/servicemanager-user-guide/reports/reports) | [My Dashboards](/servicemanager-user-guide/request-list/my-dashboard)
