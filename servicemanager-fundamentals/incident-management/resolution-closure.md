# Resolution and Closure

## Recording resolution details automatically

Where an issue resolves itself — through self-healing, a redundant or failover system, or the closure of a monitoring alert — that resolution information can be captured directly in the incident, so even automated fixes are properly recorded.

**Learn more:** [Integration Packages (monitoring & automation)](/ibridge-packages/packagelist)

## Detailed diagnosis and resolution information

The Resolve and Close action lets agents record detailed diagnosis and resolution information, including a resolution or closure category that's tracked separately from the original category, an updated component if the true cause turns out to differ from the original, and the resolution type used — giving you rich, structured resolution data for reporting.

**Learn more:** [Resolve and Close Action](/servicemanager-user-guide/service-portfolio/requests/resolve-and-close-action) | [Category Action](/servicemanager-user-guide/service-portfolio/requests/category-action)

## Notifying on resolution

Both your service desk and the affected user can be automatically notified as an incident's status changes and when it's resolved, keeping everyone up to date without extra manual steps.

**Learn more:** [Update Action](/servicemanager-user-guide/service-portfolio/requests/update-action)

## Raising related problems and changes

A problem or a change record can be raised directly from within an incident, with the two records automatically linked — so the underlying cause can be investigated, or a fix implemented, without losing the connection back to the original incident.

**Learn more:** [Link Request Action](/servicemanager-user-guide/service-portfolio/requests/link-action)

## Flexible closure options

You decide how incidents get closed, and it can be different for different types of incident. Options include the user closing (or canceling) their own request from the portal, the service desk closing manually, automatic closure after a defined period following resolution, or closure being driven by the closure of the triggering monitoring alert.

**Learn more:** [Resolve and Close Action](/servicemanager-user-guide/service-portfolio/requests/resolve-and-close-action)
