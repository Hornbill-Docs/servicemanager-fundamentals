# Major Incidents

## Recognizing major incidents

Major incidents can use their own dedicated catalog item and workflow, so they're visibly and procedurally different from routine incidents from the moment they're logged — with their own templates, priority handling, and stakeholder notifications built in.

**Learn more:** [Using Workflows with Service Manager](/servicemanager-config/customize/workflows/using-workflows-with-service-manager)

## Raising and escalating major incidents

A major incident can be raised directly, or an existing incident can be promoted once it becomes clear the impact is significant. Multiple related incidents can then be linked to the major incident record, keeping everything — and everyone affected — connected to a single point of coordination, while your workflow can apply time, service, or business-defined thresholds to trigger the escalation automatically.

**Learn more:** [Link Request Action](/servicemanager-user-guide/service-portfolio/requests/link-action) | [Update Action (linked requests)](/servicemanager-user-guide/service-portfolio/requests/update-action)
